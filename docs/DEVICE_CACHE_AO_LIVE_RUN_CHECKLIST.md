Two timing rules that explain what you will see:
- The app saves a copy only after a full read of a workbook that was last changed MORE THAN 10 MINUTES before the read (was 2; raised after step 7 showed Excel serving old content for minutes). Right after any edit (yours, Copilot's or the app's), reads are used but no copy is saved; a later read saves it.
- A launch trusts the copy only if the workbook has not changed in the LAST 10 MINUTES. Within 10 minutes of any edit, a launch shows the copy briefly and then re-reads the workbook.

So for steps 5 and 6, leave the workbook alone for at least 10 minutes first.

4. First launch of this build (a new build deletes any old copy). Open the TEST build normally. Expect: the usual splash. In Docket > About: "Data: read from the workbook HH:MM", "Launch read (…): 11 of 11 sheets, …". If the workbook changed within the last 10 minutes, swipe the app away, wait until it is 10+ minutes since the change and open it again before step 5 (so a copy gets saved).
5. Launch from the copy. Swipe the app away and open it again (workbook untouched for 10+ minutes). Expect: the splash goes noticeably faster. Docket > About: "Data: checked with the workbook HH:MM (saved copy matched)", "Saved copy at launch (…): matched the workbook in X s; no sheet reads", "Launch read: none (the saved copy was used)". Browse, Albums and a coin's details look as usual. Edit Coin works (its Save is not dimmed).
6. A change made elsewhere, at launch. Swipe the app away. In Excel, change something visible on a _Testing coin (for example its Remarks). Open the app right away. Expect: no gray banner any more (Ray, 7 Nov); the coin shows the new value once Excel has it (see step 7b: Excel can take minutes to serve new content). Docket > About: "Saved copy at launch (…): workbook changed; re-read". Put the Excel value back.
7. A change made elsewhere while the app is open. With the app open, change a coin in Excel. Switch to another app for at least a minute, then come back (repeat every minute or two). Expect: NO toast while Excel still serves the old content; the toast "Updated" only on the return when the new value actually appears. Put it back.

Expect: the row goes, toast "Back online" (or "Back online · updated"); Save now works; after it, Details shows the new value straight away and, if you open Edit Coin again, the form shows the new value with Save NOT highlighted (B2), even while Excel still serves the old content.
